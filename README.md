## The line

> I tried to meditate. My brain treated it as an open mic night.

62 characters. The joke: meditation promises a quiet mind; the narrator's
brain does the opposite — it treats the silence as a stage and performs.
The meditation angle was unseen in the field (391-title check); the
saturated smart-home/AI-device, plant, gym-ghosting, 2 a.m. snack, landlord,
hotel-shower, cheese-interview and ATM-receipt angles were all avoided.

## How it runs

`python3 main.py` writes the single line to `/output/entry.txt` — no
trailing newline, mode 644, byte-identical on every run. Pure Python
standard library, no network, no randomness.
